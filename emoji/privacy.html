Emoji Studio · Privacy Policy

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 1 日

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Emoji Studio 是一个完全本地运行的表情贴纸与 GIF 创作工作室。绘画、动画、表情工厂生成与所有导出（PNG / GIF / 精灵图 / ZIP）均在你的设备浏览器内完成，不上传任何创作内容。设计目标是尽量少收集数据。

一、在你设备上处理的内容

  • 画布作品、动画帧、贴纸草稿与最近编辑记录（chrome.storage.local）
  • 设置：界面语言、画布偏好、解锁状态缓存
  • 表情工厂中你输入的文案、以及（Pro）上传的自定义图片：仅在本机用于生成贴纸，随作品保存在本地，不会上传
  • 可选 Pro 解锁经 ExtensionPay：可能在本地缓存付费/邮箱信息以便短时离线识别。银行卡信息由 ExtensionPay 及其支付服务商处理

二、我们不收集

  • 你的任何画作、动画、文案或上传图片（全部留在本地）
  • 网页内容、表单数据或网站 Cookie
  • 浏览历史、标签标题或标签 URL
  • 分析或广告标识符；创作与导出过程完全离线，界面不发起远程字体或追踪请求

三、权限说明

权限用途
storage保存作品草稿、动画帧、设置与本地解锁缓存

本扩展仅申请 storage 一项权限：不注入任何网页、不读取任何页面内容，全部创作功能在扩展弹窗内离线完成。

四、网络

  • 仅在你打开购买或登录时访问 ExtensionPay（extensionpay.com）
  • 无其他自有遥测接口；绘画渲染与 GIF / ZIP 导出均在本地生成

五、数据存储与你的权利

全部数据保存在你的设备本地，可通过清除扩展数据或卸载 Emoji Studio 删除。

六、未成年人

本扩展不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

七、政策更新

我们可能不时更新本政策。重大变更可能随扩展更新说明。请定期查阅本页。

八、联系我们

隐私相关问题请发送邮件至 yugakhans@gmail.com，或联系 Chrome Web Store 商品页上的发布者。

九、同意

安装并使用 Emoji Studio，即表示你同意本隐私政策。若不同意，请勿使用本扩展。

Emoji Studio is a fully local sticker & GIF creation studio. Drawing, animation, factory generation and every export (PNG / GIF / sprite sheet / ZIP) happen inside your browser on your device. Your creations are never uploaded. It is designed to minimize data collection.

1. What we process on your device

  • Canvas artwork, animation frames, sticker drafts and recent edits (chrome.storage.local)
  • Settings: UI language, canvas preferences, local unlock cache
  • Text you type in the Sticker Factory and (Pro) images you upload: used locally only to render stickers, stored with your work on your device — never uploaded
  • Optional Pro unlock via ExtensionPay: a local paid/email cache may be kept so unlock works offline for a limited TTL. Payment card data is processed by ExtensionPay / its processors

2. What we do not collect

  • Any of your drawings, animations, captions or uploaded images (all stay local)
  • Page content, form data, or website cookies
  • Browsing history, tab titles, or tab URLs
  • Analytics or advertising identifiers; creation and export are fully offline, no remote font or tracking requests from the UI

3. Permissions

PermissionWhy
storageWork drafts, animation frames, settings and local unlock cache

The extension requests storage only — it never injects into or reads any web page; the entire studio runs offline inside the popup.

4. Network

  • ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com) only when you open purchase or login
  • No other first-party telemetry endpoints; rendering and GIF / ZIP encoding happen locally

5. Data storage and your rights

All data stays in local storage on your device. Delete it by clearing extension data or uninstalling Emoji Studio.

6. Children’s privacy

This extension is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes may be noted with extension updates. Please review this page periodically.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, email yugakhans@gmail.com or contact the publisher listed on the Chrome Web Store item page.

9. Consent

By installing and using Emoji Studio, you agree to this privacy policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the extension.
