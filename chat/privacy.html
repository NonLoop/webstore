Chat Glyph · Privacy Policy

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 1 日

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Chat Glyph 是本地运行的表情与特殊字符选择器。字符库随扩展打包，选择、搜索与插入均在你的设备上完成。设计目标是尽量少收集数据。

一、在你设备上处理的内容

  • 字符库检索、最近使用与自定义合集（chrome.storage.local）
  • 设置：语言、每日额度计数、解锁状态缓存
  • 插入动作仅在你于弹窗 / 侧边面板中主动点击字符后发生：扩展将所选字符写入当前活动输入框；无法插入时改为复制到剪贴板。扩展不读取、不上传输入框中的既有内容
  • 可选 Pro 解锁经 ExtensionPay：可能在本地缓存付费/邮箱信息以便短时离线识别。银行卡信息由 ExtensionPay 及其支付服务商处理

二、我们不收集

  • 网页内容、表单数据或网站 Cookie
  • 浏览历史、标签标题或标签 URL
  • 按键记录（仅插入你主动点击的单个字符）
  • 分析或广告标识符；扩展界面不发起远程字体或追踪请求

三、权限说明

权限用途
storage保存合集、最近使用、设置与本地解锁缓存
activeTab + scripting仅在你点击字符后，将所选字符插入当前标签页的活动输入框
sidePanel可选的常驻侧边面板（Chrome / Edge）

侧边面板与插入功能默认不活动，仅在你打开弹窗或开启面板并点击时工作。

四、网络

  • 仅在你打开购买或登录时访问 ExtensionPay（extensionpay.com）
  • 无其他自有遥测接口；字符渲染完全离线

五、数据存储与你的权利

全部数据保存在你的设备本地，可通过清除扩展数据或卸载 Chat Glyph 删除。

六、未成年人

本扩展不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

七、政策更新

我们可能不时更新本政策。重大变更可能随扩展更新说明。请定期查阅本页。

八、联系我们

隐私相关问题请发送邮件至 yugakhans@gmail.com，或联系 Chrome Web Store 商品页上的发布者。

九、同意

安装并使用 Chat Glyph，即表示你同意本隐私政策。若不同意，请勿使用本扩展。

Chat Glyph is a local emoji, kaomoji and special-character picker. The glyph library ships with the extension; browsing, search and insertion all run on your device. It is designed to minimize data collection.

1. What we process on your device

  • Glyph search, recents and custom collections (chrome.storage.local)
  • Settings: language, daily-quota counters, local unlock cache
  • Insertion happens only after you click a glyph in the popup or side panel: the chosen character is written into the active input field, or copied to the clipboard when insertion isn’t possible. Existing field content is never read or uploaded
  • Optional Pro unlock via ExtensionPay: a local paid/email cache may be kept so unlock works offline for a limited TTL. Payment card data is processed by ExtensionPay / its processors

2. What we do not collect

  • Page content, form data, or website cookies
  • Browsing history, tab titles, or tab URLs
  • Keystrokes (only the single glyph you explicitly click is inserted)
  • Analytics or advertising identifiers; no remote font or tracking requests from the UI

3. Permissions

PermissionWhy
storageCollections, recents, settings and local unlock cache
activeTab + scriptingInserting the glyph you just clicked into the active input of the current tab
sidePanelThe optional persistent side panel (Chrome / Edge)

The side panel and insertion are inactive by default — they act only when you open the popup or panel and click a glyph.

4. Network

  • ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com) only when you open purchase or login
  • No other first-party telemetry endpoints; glyph rendering is fully offline

5. Data storage and your rights

All data stays in local storage on your device. Delete it by clearing extension data or uninstalling Chat Glyph.

6. Children’s privacy

This extension is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes may be noted with extension updates. Please review this page periodically.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, email yugakhans@gmail.com or contact the publisher listed on the Chrome Web Store item page.

9. Consent

By installing and using Chat Glyph, you agree to this privacy policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the extension.
