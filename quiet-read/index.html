Features

核心能力

What’s inside

阅读、摘录、分析全部在本机完成；AI 由你自己的密钥驱动。

Reading, highlights and analysis run entirely on your device; AI is powered by your own key.

01

安静阅读模式

Calm reader mode

一键去掉杂讯，只留正文：纸白 / 米黄 / 柔光 / 天黑四主题，字号版宽可调，专注模式隐藏图片，正文内 Ctrl+F 查找，Esc 随时退出。

Strip the noise, keep the text: paper / sepia / soft-light / dusk themes, adjustable font size and measure, focus mode, in-article search, Esc to exit anytime.

02

无需 AI 的本地助手

Local assistant, no AI needed

关键句速览、字数与阅读时长、关键词与术语专名提取、文章大纲——规则全部跑在本机，不联网、零配置。

Key-sentence briefs, word count & reading time, keyword & term extraction, article outlines — rule-based, offline, zero setup.

03

划词摘录与续读记忆

Highlights & resume memory

选中即摘录，带上下文与笔记，按站点检索，导出 Markdown / JSON；每篇文章的阅读位置自动记忆，下次一键回到原处。

Select to save with context and notes, search by site, export Markdown / JSON; every article remembers where you stopped reading.

04

自带 AI Key 解锁

Bring your own AI key

在设置里填入你的 OpenAI 兼容接口地址与密钥：弹窗全文总结、工作台多轮问答、阅读模式划词「问 AI」。密钥只存在你的浏览器里，请求直达你配置的接口。

Add your OpenAI-compatible base URL and key in Settings: page summaries, multi-turn chat in the dashboard, and "Ask AI" on any selection. Your key stays in your browser; requests go straight to your endpoint.

完全免费，无内购、无账号。数据全部保存在你的设备上——阅读偏好、摘录与续读位置只存本机，不上传任何服务器。

Completely free — no in-app purchases, no accounts. All data stays on your device: preferences, highlights and reading positions never leave your browser.
