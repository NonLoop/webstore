Features

核心能力

What’s inside

实时 3D 渲染与本地造景，不需要联网也能养护一缸。

Real-time 3D rendering and a local tank — no network required to keep it alive.

01

写实生态渲染

Realistic ecosystem render

鱼群、无脊椎动物、水草与装饰同缸共生；体积水体、水面波纹、沙面焦散与光柱由引擎实时绘制。

Fish, invertebrates, plants and décor share one tank. Volume water, surface ripples, sand caustics and light shafts are drawn live by the engine.

02

随手养护

Tend it anytime

点选物种放入缸中，投喂、调节光照 / 水流 / 色温，截图保存，夜间模式一键压暗。

Place species with a tap, feed, tune light / current / color temperature, save a screenshot, and dim the tank with night mode.

03

三种打开方式

Three ways in

工具栏弹窗快速照看，独立大窗沉浸造景，可选网页右上角浮窗入口，状态在各入口之间同步。

Toolbar popup for a quick look, a full window for deeper sessions, and an optional page float — tank state stays in sync across entries.

04

本地优先

Local-first

造景、偏好与解锁缓存保存在浏览器本地；程序化音效可选，不读取、不上传你浏览的网页内容。

Layout, preferences and unlock cache stay in local browser storage. Optional procedural ambient sound. The float never reads or uploads page content.

免费可体验完整养护流程；Pro 为可选解锁，提高每种生物容量并开放跟随镜头等进阶玩法。无账号也可先玩起来。

Free covers the full care loop; Pro is an optional unlock for higher species capacity and extras like follow-cam. No account required to start.
