本隐私政策适用于 Chrome 扩展 「恋爱话术」（Love Lines）。我们以「数据留在你的浏览器里」为原则设计本扩展。

一、我们不收集的信息

本扩展不会收集、上传或出售以下内容：

  • 浏览历史、标签页标题或网页内容
  • 姓名、电子邮箱、电话等身份信息（扩展本身不要求注册）
  • 设备广告标识或行为分析埋点

扩展界面不加载分析 SDK，也不加载远程字体用于追踪。

二、仅保存在本地的数据

下列信息使用浏览器的 chrome.storage.local 仅存储在你的设备上：

  • 语言偏好与分类筛选
  • 收藏夹与复制相关的本地记录
  • 解锁 / 购买状态（用于在本机识别是否已解锁完整内容）

这些数据不会发送到我们自有的服务器。卸载扩展或清除扩展存储后，上述本地数据将被删除。

三、权限说明

本扩展仅申请 storage 权限，用于读写上述本地数据。另有内容脚本仅匹配 extensionpay.com 域名，用于完成可选的一次解锁支付流程，不会读取你浏览的其他网站。

四、第三方支付（ExtensionPay）

完整内容库的可选解锁通过 ExtensionPay 处理。支付详情由 ExtensionPay 及其支付服务商处理；本扩展不接收、不存储你的银行卡或完整支付账号信息。ExtensionPay 的数据处理遵循其自身隐私政策。

五、未成年人

本扩展面向成年用户的恋爱沟通场景设计，不面向儿童收集个人信息。

六、政策更新

若本政策变更，我们将更新本页顶部的「最后更新」日期。重大变更会通过商店商品详情或本网站提示。

七、联系我们

如对隐私或数据处理有疑问，请发邮件至 oneusop@163.com。

This Privacy Policy applies to the Chrome extension Love Lines (恋爱话术). We designed it so your data stays in your browser.

1. What We Do Not Collect

This extension does not collect, upload, or sell:

  • Browsing history, tab titles, or page content
  • Name, email, phone, or other identity details (no account is required by the extension itself)
  • Advertising identifiers or analytics / telemetry

The extension UI does not load analytics SDKs or remote fonts for tracking.

2. Data Stored Only Locally

The following is stored only on your device via chrome.storage.local:

  • Language preference and category filters
  • Favorites and local copy-related records
  • Unlock / purchase status (so this device knows whether the full library is unlocked)

This data is not sent to our own servers. Uninstalling the extension or clearing its storage deletes this local data.

3. Permissions

The extension requests only the storage permission for the local data above. A content script runs solely on extensionpay.com to complete the optional one-time unlock flow; it does not read other websites you browse.

4. Third-Party Payments (ExtensionPay)

Optional unlock of the full library is handled by ExtensionPay. Payment details are processed by ExtensionPay and its payment providers; this extension does not receive or store your card numbers or full payment account details. ExtensionPay’s handling of data is governed by its own privacy policy.

5. Children’s Privacy

The extension is designed for adult dating-communication use cases and does not collect personal information from children.

6. Policy Updates

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date above. Material changes may also be noted on the store listing or this site.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy or data handling? Email oneusop@163.com.
