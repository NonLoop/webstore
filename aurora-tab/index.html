What’s inside

One tab that greets you with time, focus and style — and renders instantly, every time.

打开新标签页，时间、专注与风格一起抵达——每次都即时渲染，绝无等待。

01

19 draggable widgets

19 个可拖拽组件

Clock, search, quick links, AI navigation, music & focus sounds, notes, todos, pomodoro, weather, countdown, quote, habits, world clock, calendar, currency, calculator, water tracker, stopwatch — arrange them your way with a single edit mode.

时钟、搜索、常用网址、AI 导航、听歌与专注音效、记事本、待办、番茄钟、天气、倒计时、每日一言、习惯打卡、世界时钟、月历、汇率、计算器、喝水打卡、秒表——一个编辑模式，随心排列。

02

6 search engines, one box

一个搜索框，六大引擎

Google, Baidu, Bing, DuckDuckGo, Sogou and 360 switch with one tap; the default is remembered across sessions. URLs open directly.

Google、百度、Bing、DuckDuckGo、搜狗、360 一键切换，默认引擎跨会话记忆；输入网址则直接打开。

03

8 skins + your wallpaper

8 款皮肤 + 自定义壁纸

Aurora, ocean, mono, sakura, sunrise, forest, starry and rosegold themes retint the whole UI — cards, drawers and controls included. Upload any image as your own background.

极光、深海、极简、樱花、日出、森林、星空、玫瑰金，整站换色——卡片、抽屉与控件一并跟随。还能上传任意图片作为专属壁纸。

04

Focus sounds & toolbox, all local

专注音效 + 工具箱，全本地

Nine synthesized soundscapes (rain, waves, campfire, birds…) are generated with WebAudio — no files, no streaming. A toolbox covers text utilities, unit conversion, generators and encoders. UI in Simplified / Traditional Chinese and English.

九种合成音景（雨声、海浪、篝火、鸟鸣……）由 WebAudio 纯本地生成——没有音频文件、没有流媒体。工具箱涵盖文本处理、单位换算、随机生成与编解码。界面支持简体、繁體與 English。

Everything essential is free — drag layout, 6 engines, 3 skins, all core widgets. Aurora VIP is a one-time unlock (cached locally) for all 8 skins, wallpaper, premium widgets and backup tools. No subscription, no ads, no tracking.

核心能力全部免费——拖拽布局、6 大引擎、3 款皮肤与所有基础组件。Aurora VIP 一次性解锁（本地缓存生效），包含全部 8 款皮肤、壁纸、高级组件与备份工具。无订阅、无广告、无追踪。
