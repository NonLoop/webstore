AI Math Kit 是数论未解问题与 AI / 形式化数学突破导读扩展。题库、突破导读与提示词模板随扩展打包、离线可读。扩展本身没有自有服务器，也不收集你的浏览内容。

一、在你设备上处理的内容

  • 界面语言与偏好设置（仅存 chrome.storage.local）
  • 可选 Pro 解锁经 ExtensionPay：可能在本地缓存付费状态 / 邮箱，以便短时离线识别（已付费约 15 天、未付费约 7 天 TTL）。银行卡信息由 ExtensionPay 及其支付服务商处理
  • 题库搜索与筛选在本地完成，搜索词不会上传

二、我们不收集

  • 网页内容、表单数据或网站 Cookie
  • 浏览历史、标签标题或标签 URL
  • 分析或广告标识符；商店落地页以外的扩展 UI 不发起追踪请求
  • 你的对话内容：本扩展不内置云端 AI 对话通道；你复制提示词到第三方 AI 时，受该服务商自己的政策约束

三、权限说明

权限用途
storage保存语言偏好与本地支付状态缓存
主机权限（仅 extensionpay.com）在购买 / 登录页注入 ExtensionPay 脚本以完成解锁

题库与突破内容全部打包在扩展内离线使用；阅读本身不产生网络请求。

四、网络汇总

  • ExtensionPay（extensionpay.com）——仅在你打开购买、登录或强制刷新支付状态时
  • 除此之外无任何自有分析 / 广告请求；知识内容完全离线

五、数据存储与你的权利

偏好与支付缓存保存在你的设备本地，可通过清除扩展数据或卸载 AI Math Kit 删除。经 ExtensionPay 处理的支付数据，请参照其政策行使权利。

六、未成年人

本扩展不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

七、政策更新

我们可能不时更新本政策。重大变更可能随扩展更新说明。请定期查阅本页。

八、联系我们与同意

隐私相关问题请发送邮件至 yugakhans@gmail.com，或联系 Chrome Web Store 商品页上的发布者。安装并使用 AI Math Kit，即表示你同意本隐私政策；若不同意，请勿使用本扩展。

AI Math Kit is a browser extension for number-theory open problems and AI / formalization breakthrough guides. The problem bank, guides and prompt templates ship with the extension and read offline. The extension has no first-party server and does not collect your browsing content.

1. What we process on your device

  • UI language and preferences (stored in chrome.storage.local only)
  • Optional Pro unlock via ExtensionPay: a local paid/email cache may be kept so unlock works offline for a limited TTL (~15 days if paid / ~7 days if unpaid). Payment card data is processed by ExtensionPay / its processors
  • Search and filtering run locally; search terms are never uploaded

2. What we do not collect

  • Page content, form data, or website cookies
  • Browsing history, tab titles, or tab URLs
  • Analytics or advertising identifiers; the extension UI does not make tracking requests
  • Your chat content: this extension has no built-in cloud AI channel; if you paste prompts into a third-party AI, that provider’s policy applies

3. Permissions

PermissionWhy
storageLanguage prefs and local payment-status cache
Host permission (only extensionpay.com)Inject ExtensionPay scripts on purchase / login pages to complete unlock

Problem and breakthrough content is bundled offline; reading it generates no network requests.

4. Network summary

  • ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com) — only when you open purchase, login, or force-refresh payment status
  • No other first-party analytics / ads; content is fully offline

5. Data storage and your rights

Preferences and payment cache stay in local storage on your device — delete them by clearing extension data or uninstalling AI Math Kit. For payment data handled by ExtensionPay, exercise your rights under its policy.

6. Children’s privacy

This extension is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes may be noted with extension updates. Please review this page periodically.

8. Contact and consent

For privacy questions, email yugakhans@gmail.com or contact the publisher listed on the Chrome Web Store item page. By installing and using AI Math Kit, you agree to this privacy policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the extension.
