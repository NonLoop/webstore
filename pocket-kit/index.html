What’s inside

A whole arcade and workstation living in your toolbar — zero network required.

一整间街机厅和工具箱住在你的工具栏里，完全不需要联网。

01

Offline mini-games

离线小游戏

Short sessions built around reaction, memory, numbers, and arcade-style play—instant start, no loading screens, high scores stay on your device.

按「几分钟一局」挑选，涵盖反应、记忆、数字和街机等常见玩法；即点即玩，没有加载页，最高分只留在本机。

02

Local utilities

本地实用工具

Everyday text, encoding, time, and number helpers that finish the small chores without leaving the tab—every calculation happens locally.

文本处理、编码转换、时间与数值计算等日常小麻烦，就地完成；全部在本地计算，不离开当前标签页。

03

Scores & recents, on device

成绩与最近使用，只留本地

High scores, best-lowest records and your recently used items are cached in chrome.storage — nothing is uploaded, ever.

最高分、最少步纪录与最近使用都保存在浏览器本地存储中，永不上传。

Trilingual, with a floating entry

Full zh / en / ja UI with a built-in language switcher. Optionally pin a small floating launcher on any page — it never reads page content.
