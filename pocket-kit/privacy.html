Pocket Kit · Privacy Policy

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 1 日

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Pocket Kit 是完全离线运行的迷你游戏与实用工具集。所有游戏逻辑与工具计算（密码生成、哈希、换算、正则等）均在你的设备浏览器内完成，无需联网，也不上传任何数据。设计目标是尽量少收集数据。

一、在你设备上处理的内容

  • 最高分、最少步纪录、最近使用与收藏（chrome.storage.local）
  • 设置：界面语言（中 / 英 / 日）、浮动入口开关、解锁状态缓存
  • 工具中输入的文本（如待哈希字符串、正则测试样本）：仅在本机处理，不存储、不上传，除非你主动点击复制
  • 可选 Pro 解锁经 ExtensionPay：可能在本地缓存付费/邮箱信息以便短时离线识别。银行卡信息由 ExtensionPay 及其支付服务商处理

二、我们不收集

  • 网页内容、表单数据或网站 Cookie
  • 浏览历史、标签标题或标签 URL
  • 你的游戏数据或工具输入输出（全部留在本地）
  • 分析或广告标识符；游戏与工具界面完全离线

三、权限说明

权限用途
storage保存分数、最近使用、设置与本地解锁缓存
主机权限 http://*/*、https://*/*仅用于可选的浮动入口：在你开启该功能后，在页面上放置一个小按钮，点击即打开扩展。浮窗不读取、不记录任何页面内容；关闭该功能后不再注入

浮动入口默认关闭，且其注入内容为一个独立的按钮与弹窗，不会采集周围页面的任何文字、表单或 Cookie。

四、网络

  • 仅在你打开购买或登录时访问 ExtensionPay（extensionpay.com）
  • 无其他自有遥测接口；全部 33 个游戏与工具均离线可用

五、数据存储与你的权利

全部数据保存在你的设备本地，可通过清除扩展数据或卸载 Pocket Kit 删除。

六、未成年人

本扩展不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集儿童的个人信息。

七、政策更新

我们可能不时更新本政策。重大变更可能随扩展更新说明。请定期查阅本页。

八、联系我们

隐私相关问题请发送邮件至 yugakhans@gmail.com，或联系 Chrome Web Store 商品页上的发布者。

九、同意

安装并使用 Pocket Kit，即表示你同意本隐私政策。若不同意，请勿使用本扩展。

Pocket Kit is a fully offline collection of mini-games and practical tools. All game logic and tool computations (password generation, hashing, conversions, regex testing, etc.) run inside your browser on your device — no network connection is needed and no data is uploaded. It is designed to minimize data collection.

1. What we process on your device

  • High scores, best-lowest records, recents and favorites (chrome.storage.local)
  • Settings: UI language (zh / en / ja), floating-entry toggle, local unlock cache
  • Text you type into tools (strings to hash, regex samples, etc.): processed on your machine only, never stored or uploaded unless you explicitly copy the result
  • Optional Pro unlock via ExtensionPay: a local paid/email cache may be kept so unlock works offline for a limited TTL. Payment card data is processed by ExtensionPay / its processors

2. What we do not collect

  • Page content, form data, or website cookies
  • Browsing history, tab titles, or tab URLs
  • Your game data or tool inputs/outputs (all stay local)
  • Analytics or advertising identifiers; games and tools work completely offline

3. Permissions

PermissionWhy
storageScores, recents, settings and local unlock cache
Host permissions http://*/*, https://*/*Used only for the optional floating entry: when you enable it, a small button is placed on the page that opens the extension when clicked. The overlay never reads or records page content; disabling the feature stops all injection

The floating entry is off by default, and what it injects is an isolated button + panel — it collects no text, forms or cookies from the surrounding page.

4. Network

  • ExtensionPay (extensionpay.com) only when you open purchase or login
  • No other first-party telemetry endpoints; all 33 games and tools work offline

5. Data storage and your rights

All data stays in local storage on your device. Delete it by clearing extension data or uninstalling Pocket Kit.

6. Children’s privacy

This extension is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Significant changes may be noted with extension updates. Please review this page periodically.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, email yugakhans@gmail.com or contact the publisher listed on the Chrome Web Store item page.

9. Consent

By installing and using Pocket Kit, you agree to this privacy policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the extension.
